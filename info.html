KEEP THE RHYTHM RESPECTFUL

Community guidelines

A welcoming music community depends on how we treat one another. Help make this a space where people can connect and create safely.

01

Respect every member

Be kind across differences in culture, country, experience, genre, and identity. Harassment, hate speech, threats, and discriminatory behavior are not welcome.

02

Keep it honest

Represent yourself and your opportunities accurately. Do not impersonate others, mislead members, or post fraudulent listings.

03

Share responsibly

Post content you have permission to share. Respect other people's privacy, creative work, and boundaries.

04

Protect your safety

Do not post passwords, payment details, private account information, or someone else's personal information. Verify arrangements before meeting or paying.

05

Help keep spaces useful

Stay on topic, avoid spam, and communicate clearly. If something feels unsafe or violates these guidelines, contact your community administrator.

YOUR INFORMATION, YOUR CHOICES

Privacy at a glance

Your account information and the profile details you choose to share serve different purposes. Review what is visible before adding information to your public profile or posts.

⌑

Account email

Your sign-in email is private from other members and is visible only to you and authorized administrators.

◎

Country

The country you select when joining is used for administration and is not shown as a public profile detail.

◉

Public profile

Your name, photo, bio, and any contact details you choose to share may be visible to community members.

⌖

Location and weather

Location access is optional. Weather lookup uses your chosen place or device location for the weather service; it is not added to your musician profile.

For full account and data questions, contact your community administrator. Never share your password or one-time recovery codes with anyone.

NEED A HAND?

Help & frequently asked questions

Search the answers or choose a topic. For account-specific help, sign in and contact your community administrator.

Who can join Musicians World?

Musicians and music lovers from around the world can request an account. Select your country when signing up. New accounts may require administrator approval before you can sign in.

How do I create an account?

Choose “Join the community” or “Create a viewer account” and complete the form with your name, email, country, username, and password. Use a unique password of at least 12 characters.

What can I do after signing in?

You can share with the community, discover musicians, browse gigs, listen to shared music, join chat rooms, and explore the instrument marketplace.

How do I get help with a gig or community listing?

Contact the member who posted it and confirm the details directly. Listings are member-created and are not independently verified or guaranteed by the platform.

Can other members see my account email or country?

Your account email and registration country are private from other members. Authorized administrators may access account details for platform administration. Contact details you add to a public profile or listing may be visible to members.

What should I do if I feel unsafe?

Stop the interaction, avoid sharing more personal or payment information, and contact your community administrator. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

What if I cannot sign in?

Use the password recovery option on the sign-in screen to request a one-time code by email. If you cannot access your account email or do not receive a code, contact your administrator.
